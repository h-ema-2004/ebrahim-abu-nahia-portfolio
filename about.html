About me

Curiosity, persistence, engineering.

I build practical applications that solve real problems and make complex tasks feel simpler.

I'm Ebrahim AbuNahia, a fourth-year Computer Systems Engineering student and software developer from Gaza, Palestine. I enjoy taking an idea through problem definition, system design, user experience, implementation and testing.

My projects span offline-first retail and ledger systems, Windows desktop software, Firebase-backed booking platforms and small product prototypes. I prefer clear architecture, accountable feature descriptions and working solutions over inflated claims.

Continuing my studies and development work through the war in Gaza has made reliability, accessibility and resourcefulness especially meaningful to me.
