Product case study · November 2026

Sound Packs: let sounds speak.

An independent feature proposal for quick, reusable, audio-only chat reactions—created in Gaza and shared with WhatsApp for consideration. This project is not associated with, endorsed by or integrated into WhatsApp or Meta.

Try the prototype ↓Back to projects

The opportunity

A lightweight language of reactions.

Short sound memes can express tone, humor and cultural references that don't always fit a text message or an image-based sticker. Sound Packs explores a reusable sound-only format in its own right.

  • Record or import an audio reaction.
  • Organize short clips into personal packs.
  • Preview, favorite and send with one tap.
  • Share packs as collections, with user-controlled playback.

From idea to interaction

Try the prototype.

Browser-based simulated messaging. No messages are sent to real WhatsApp accounts; microphone access may require HTTPS.

Prototype only: no platform endorsement, real account connection or production messaging functionality. Demo sounds are synthesized; user audio stays in the browser.

Product principles

Quick, optional and respectful.

Playback should be initiated by the recipient. A production implementation would also need safety and reporting controls, accessibility, copyright handling, user privacy and sensible duration limits.

Technical scope

Honest proof of concept.

This prototype demonstrates UI flows and local browser behavior. It does not claim to implement WhatsApp transport, end-to-end encryption, server-side pack sharing or real-time delivery.
